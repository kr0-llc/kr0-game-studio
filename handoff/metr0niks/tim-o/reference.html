Tim-o

The reference mech: a salvaged heavy-duty resistance unit with a round torso, one eye, a pile-bunker arm and a minigun arm. Every new Tim-o image starts from the two canon images below. kr0 confirmed them as canon on 31 October 2026.

Keep in every image

  • One round lens eye set high in the spherical torso. There is no separate head.
  • Spherical peach torso with sage plates and small vents; sand ball joints at the shoulders, knees and elbows.
  • Arms: a pile-bunker spike on one arm, a six-barrel rotary minigun on the other. The game mirrors sides in code.
  • Reactor pack covering most of the back, two tall cylinders with round lights rising above the shoulders.
  • Stocky legs with round sage knee pads and wide feet, planted apart.
  • Clean ink line, flat cel shading, no text or background in the image.
