Three districts, one landmark each

Today the Underpass, Freight Yard and Smelter Row are the same recipe: rows of 2 to 5 m blocks north and south of the deck, six brick hues cycled in order, a barrier and a container every few metres. There were three concepts; kr0 picked A with one landmark from B in each zone. The first tab shows that map as built in sim.js; the others are the concepts as proposed.

Picked by kr0, Oct 28

A as the base, with one landmark from B in each zone

Districts give every zone a look you can read at a glance, which is the problem you raised. A single landmark per zone (water tower, gantry crane, slag gallery) gives each zone a moment that pays off "the city is a weapon" before and after the overpass hook. C's pour floor is a strong idea for the last level band, but it removes a pillar pair and changes the GDD's overpass rule, so I would hold it for later.

After you pick

  1. The full map at block level: every building, barrier, container and prop with coordinates, as the new MAP data for sim.js.
  2. A breakdown per section (each zone split into north street, under the deck and south street): purpose, sightlines, cover, sniper perches, pod and spawn areas, and the collapse hooks.
  3. Pacing notes per level band, so levels 1, 2 and 3+ each open on something new.
  4. Then the voxel prototype plays it, with screenshots of each zone.
