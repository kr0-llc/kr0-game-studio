Metr0niks

Prototype: Overpass Sector 4, a few blocks under the highway. Patrol bots drop in and hunt you. Drive with your legs, aim with your torso, and keep the minigun from overheating. The city is a weapon: break two pillars in a row and the road between them falls on whoever is underneath. A run is five waves with a Bounty sniper on waves 3 and 5: clear wave 5 to extract and bank your scrap, die and the match's scrap is gone. Drop pods land with a cyan marker: touch one for armor or a faster pile-bunker, but the bots want them too. Heavy loaders do not shoot: they telegraph, then ram in a straight line, plowing through barriers and containers. The minigun barely scratches their armor; the pile-bunker does not.
