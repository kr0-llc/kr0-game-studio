# Metr0niks fun pass, Oct 28

Oct 28, 2026 · game-designer, for kr0 · decisions GDD-D35 to GDD-D50; round 2 below records kr0's answers

kr0 asked for another round of GDD improvements and expansion to make the game more fun. This record says what is weak today, what each proposal changes, which pillar it serves, and the order I would build them in. Every proposal is folded into the [GDD](../gdd.md) and tagged "(proposed, GDD-Dxx)" where it appears, so kr0 can accept or reject them one at a time. Numbers are starting values for sweeps, not tuned values ([tuning history](../tuning-history.md)). The previous GDD is kept in [superseded/gdd-2026-10-27.md](../superseded/gdd-2026-10-27.md).

Nothing here touches the decided frame: PC with keyboard and mouse only (GDD-D29), Excursion as the only mode (GDD-D17), no early cash-out (GDD-D8), the death reset to level 1 (GDD-D14), one boss with a wave-5 variant (GDD-D13), the host owns the co-op campaign (GDD-D31), and both prototypes reading the same rules from `sim.js` (GDD-D32). Map layouts are being designed in a separate thread; this pass only adds rules for the props that thread will place.

## Where the fun is thin today

1. **Levels 4 to 50 are the same match with bigger numbers.** After level 3 opens the last zone, a level only changes counts, health, damage and scrap. With three enemy types and one map, the climb back after a death replays what you already beat. The research calls this the treadmill risk ([research](../research/mech-games-2026-10-26.md), principle 9 and the "repetitive missions" pitfall).
2. **A Perfect is only a damage number.** The ring is the game's signature, but a Perfect does the same thing as two Goods. Nothing builds across a fight, and nothing rewards a run of good timing (principles 6 and 7).
3. **Heat is a hard stop.** At 100 you lose your guns for 3 s with no choice to make. The research suggests heat should push choices, not punish (principle 2).
4. **The extraction tension peaks once and has no decision in it.** Wave 5 clears and you extract after 1.4 s. You are never asked to bet the wallet (principle 10). GDD-D8 rules out cashing out early, but it doesn't rule out pushing on.
5. **The city stops being a weapon late in the campaign.** Collapses deal a flat 80 to enemies while enemy health grows 15% a level, so by level 20 a span drop no longer kills a bot (Oct 26 critique, open questions for the team). Only the overpass and buildings fall; the three zones play alike.
6. **Every shopping trip ends.** Once the 21 parts are owned, the garage has nothing new to show, and nothing tells you what the next level will ask of your build (principle 1).
7. **Co-op players fight side by side but never together.** No rule rewards two mechs hitting the same target (principle 8).
8. **Pods are a coin flip between two heals.** Repair or Overclock; the race is the only interesting part.

### How the scrap bonuses combine (part of GDD-D35, D37, D40, D41, D44)

An enemy's cubes = its base cubes × the level scale × 2 for a Perfect pile-bunker kill × 2 in Overtime × 3 for an Elite × (1 + every percentage bonus that applies, added together), rounded. The percentage bonuses are the beat chain (20%, 40% or 60%), the demolition bonus (50%) and Crossfire (50%). So the bonuses add with each other and multiply the doublings, which keeps the top end in reach of a sweep instead of running away.

## Build order I recommend

1. **Overtime, contracts and sector conditions** (GDD-D37, D38, D39 without Blackout). The cheapest change with the biggest effect on variety: rules in `sim.js`, a garage preview and a HUD line. Every level after this feels different, before any new art.
2. **Beat chain, stagger, Crossfire and feel** (GDD-D35, D44, D45). Makes the ring the star of every fight.
3. **Collapse scaling, demolition bonus and the zone props** (GDD-D41), together with the level design thread's layouts.
4. **Scuttlers and Wreckers** (part of GDD-D40). The two enemies that change the most about how a wave plays.
5. **New pods** (GDD-D43).
6. **New parts and blueprints** (GDD-D42), once bands exist to unlock them.
7. **Redline, Bulwarks and Elites** (GDD-D36, the rest of D40), last, because they most need sweeps.

## Pillar check

Each proposal names its pillar in the table above. Two notes for the creative-director gate:

- Redline (GDD-D36) bends "every part is a tradeoff" toward "every second is a tradeoff". If kr0 prefers heat as a hard stop, drop D36 and the Redline reactor and Hot hands contract go with it.
- Overtime (GDD-D37) keeps GDD-D8 intact: there is still no way to bank before wave 5, only a way to risk more after it.

## Considered and not proposed

- **Extract after any wave** (the research's suggestion): GDD-D8 already rejected it.
- **Checkpoints every 10 levels:** would break "lose the run, keep the garage". Blueprints (GDD-D42) give the climb back its novelty instead.
- **A second boss:** GDD-D13 chose one boss for cost. Sniper's nest (two snipers on wave 5) and Elites add boss-wave variety without a new model. A second boss stays an open question.
- **Friendly fire, procedural maps, damage that carries between matches, PvP:** non-goals.
- **Changing the level scaling itself:** whether level 50 is reachable is a sweep question for the tuning history, not a fun rule. Threat bands add enemies on top of today's scaling, so the sweep has to cover both.

## A draft for "What makes it fun"

The GDD keeps this TBD on kr0's instruction (GDD-D27). If kr0 wants a starting line, this is the one these proposals are built around:

> Bringing the city down on a crowd, landing the heavy hit on the beat, and deciding, with a full wallet, whether to push one more wave.
