# Metr0niks GDD decision history

Newest first. Status is Proposed until kr0 accepts or rejects it. The [GDD](gdd.md) holds the rules; this file records the choices behind them so debates don't repeat. The reviews that raised each question are in [reviews/](reviews/SYNTHESIS.md). Decisions made on 2026-10-20 are in the table in [open questions](open-questions.md) and are not repeated here.

| ID | Date | Decision | Options considered | Why | Status | Decided by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GDD-D20 | 2026-10-25 | New values the GDD sets so no rule is left open: rejoin window 60 s, with the held mech taking no damage and the match paused in single-player; wave-5 boss with 1.5× health, a 1.5 s fire interval and 18 scrap; input locked and no damage during a zone drop-in; co-op at the lower level | Leave them TBD; ask kr0 for each | The decisions of Oct 25 needed numbers the prototype lacks | Proposed | |
| GDD-D19 | 2026-10-25 | The GDD drops what the prototype has no version of: accessibility options (timing-window widening, auto-release rings) and remappable keys, the overheat steam zone, armored transports, vaults and power relays, the Smelting Yards and Neon Financial Plaza maps, the Coolant flood and Bolt-on plate pods, the buried-pod rule, free torso pitch, the two-heavy-weapons edge case and enemies breaking supports on purpose. They are listed in open questions as a backlog | Keep them in the GDD with TBD values; add them to the prototype | kr0: use prototype values, drop the rest | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D18 | 2026-10-25 | Durability rules in full: on extract each fitted non-starter part gains wear of 10% of the armor lost divided by max armor; wear cuts every helpful stat change of the part by that share; starter parts never wear; repair costs price × wear × 25%, 50% or 75% for common, rare and epic; armor refills every level; no wear on a death. This replaces the prototype's carried armor | Cut durability; keep the carried armor pool with a tier-priced repair | Turns GDD-D9 into rules with numbers | Proposed (rules and numbers) | |
| GDD-D17 | 2026-10-25 | Excursion is the only game mode. Gauntlet, Draft Duel and Scrap Rush leave the GDD, the TDD and the prototype, and the "PvP game" wording goes with them. This supersedes GDD-D7 and the identity part of GDD-D6 | Keep Gauntlet as practice; keep the PvP modes as later modes | One mode keeps the GDD, economy and prototype consistent | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D16 | 2026-10-25 | The garage sells the prototype's 21 parts on three rarity tiers, written as stat changes. Stat upgrades and higher-tier weapons are not in the first version | Keep the five-tier, 12-part table; keep stat upgrades and higher-tier weapons as extra sinks | The prototype already uses stat changes and one upward track; the review found four overlapping power tracks and two sources of truth | Proposed (kr0 approved the rewrite, not this cut) | |
| GDD-D15 | 2026-10-25 | Heat and timing follow the prototype: every timing result costs the same heat, an overheat during a ring adds none, the Cryo flash-vent ends a lockout, Overclock doubles every fill for 15 s. Collapse shadows and pod markers draw above the low-health vignette | Keep the original table (a Miss costs full heat); make a Miss cheaper | A cheaper Miss would make letting a ring close a low-heat fire mode; the GDD text was out of date | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D14 | 2026-10-25 | Excursion is the game's main mode, played single-player or co-op, and is not called "story mode". Dying resets the campaign to level 1, keeping parts and banked scrap. Gauntlet also resets to level 1. Co-op plays at the lower of the two players' levels. This replaces GDD-D3 | Keep the level and zones after a death; reset only Gauntlet | Matches the prototype; keeps zones as a climb that restarts | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D13 | 2026-10-25 | The same sniper appears on waves 3 and 5; wave 5 is a harder variant on a skyline the player has wrecked | A simpler wave-3 boss with the sniper as the wave-5 climax | A second boss is the costliest asset; the prototype already spawns the sniper twice | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D12 | 2026-10-25 | Damage regions (disabled arms and legs) are deferred; the first version has one armor pool, as prototyped. The ram fallback and the capped weight gain wait with them | Build regions now with the ram and weight-cap rules | The prototype has no regions; they carry the soft-lock and weight-exploit findings | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D11 | 2026-10-25 | Every mech can ram for melee damage; a disabled weapon's weight comes off but the speed gain is capped below a healthy build's and the HUD explains it | Self-destruct option; keep a disabled weapon as dead weight; cut the weight rule; leave as is | A mech with no arms must never be helpless; a stripped last stand is worth keeping if it can't beat a healthy build | Deferred with regions (GDD-D12); not in the GDD | kr0 |
| GDD-D10 | 2026-10-25 | A disconnect is not leaving early: the player can rejoin within a window (length TBD) and their mech holds until they do | A disconnect is a loss without penalty; a disconnect is the same as leaving early | Always-online play would otherwise lose a whole run to a dropped connection | Applied; not built (prototype changes) | kr0 |
| GDD-D9 | 2026-10-25 | Durability stays in the first version with a floor: starter parts repair free; other parts take durability damage and cost more to repair at higher tiers | Cut durability from the first version; keep it on the single armor pool | Without a floor, all-or-nothing scrap and durability make a death spiral | Applied; rules in GDD-D18 | kr0 |
| GDD-D8 | 2026-10-25 | There is no cash-out after the wave-3 boss: scrap is all-or-nothing until extract | An opt-in extract at wave 3 at a lower rate; an automatic partial bank | An automatic bank invites a suicide-farm loop; kr0 kept the extraction tension | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D7 | 2026-10-25 | Gauntlet is practice: its scrap never banks, and its rewards are mastery goals and test-driving unowned parts (details TBD) | A repair-only scrap pool; a capped or reduced rate | Banked Gauntlet scrap would beat Excursion; the prototype currently banks it and has to change | Superseded by GDD-D17 | kr0 |
| GDD-D6 | 2026-10-25 | Metr0niks is a PvP mech game shipping a co-op PvE stage first; Draft Duel and Scrap Rush are the destination | A PvE extraction game that may add PvP later | The pillars and pitch lines are written for mech-versus-mech play; online-only fits the PvP game | Applied, then the PvP wording was dropped by GDD-D17 | kr0 |
| GDD-D5 | 2026-10-25 | The first version stays always-online. The GDD states why (proposed: account-bound progression shared by co-op and later PvP, and no edited saves carried into PvP) and defines disconnects | Offline-capable single-player with cloud saves | Matches the PvP direction; offline progress would have to be kept out of PvP accounts | Applied; reason is Proposed | kr0 |
| GDD-D4 | 2026-10-25 | Co-op (two player mechs) ships in the first version. "No multiplayer" leaves the non-goals; "no PvP" replaces it | Single-player first, with the two-player zone wording cut | The prototype already runs co-op on a server-run sim | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D3 | 2026-10-25 | Zones are an account unlock: extracting opens the next zone for good and a death doesn't close it | Zones open across waves in each excursion; cut zones | Gives an onboarding ramp across sessions | Superseded by GDD-D14 the same day | kr0 |
| GDD-D2 | 2026-10-25 | Rewrite the GDD from the Oct 20 version, applying these decisions and the current prototype. The Oct 20 version moves to `superseded/` | Patch the old GDD in place | Several sections contradicted each other and the prototype; the old version is kept | Applied | kr0 |
| GDD-D1 | 2026-10-25 | Review the GDD with six reviewer roles in three rounds (the gdd-review-kit method), reading only the GDD, then reconcile with the prototype | Review GDD plus TDD and pitch; a review of the GDD alone | A fresh read of the document by itself shows what a new reader would find | Applied | kr0 |
