# Metr0niks Puppet Rigging Lab

Published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/3mHrUSYnuyBu1uvzrdpaPZ (private to kr0). Named the Pivot Bench until 29 Oct 2026, when kr0 renamed it.

kr0 places pivot and joint marks on puppet parts and reviews each part; Claude reads both back and applies them.

- **Rigs**: Tim-o v4 (ten parts from the exploded sheet `art/metr0niks/characters/tim-o/puppet/exploded-v1.*`, own thigh and shin per leg, pivots seeded from the caps; opens first), Tim-o v3 (v2.1 torso, legs and pack with the v3 arms split at the elbow), Tim-o v2.1, and the ink enemies (gunner bot, loader, boss) from `art/metr0niks/ink-proto/sprites/atlas.*`.
- **Pivot**: the point a part turns around; it is placed on its parent's joint. **Joint**: a point on a part where a child part's pivot goes (shares of the part's box, 0 to 1). **Rest angle**: the part's built-in rotation (`rot`), only on parts nothing else depends on.
- **Preview**: the rig is drawn with the same maths as `games/metr0niks/prototypes/prototype-ink.html` (`drawBiped`, `drawCrab`, `drawSpider`): IK legs, aim clamps and the code's leg nudges. Tim-o v3 adds two-bone arms that the game does not have yet: the upper arm drops by an elbow bend and the forearm comes back up to the aim (bend 0.8 rad at rest, swinging while walking, 0.35 while aiming). The forearm is drawn under the upper arm so the elbow socket covers its cap.
- **Saved marks**: database collection `pivots`, one document per rig (`timo4`, `timo3`, `timo`, `gunner`, `loader`, `boss`) with `parts.<sprite>.{pivot, joints, rot, note}`, `edited`, `note`, `updatedAt`. Rigs that share a sprite (Tim-o v3 and v2.1 share torso, legs and pack) load oldest first, so the newest marks win. `pivots-archive/timo-2026-10-28` holds trial marks kr0 made before saying no pivots were corrected yet.
- **Part reviews**: collection `reviews`, one document per sprite: `verdict` (Keep, Fix, Redo or null), `note`, `rig`, `part`, `updatedAt`. Each part card also opens a page comment.
- **Applying**: Tim-o goes into `art/metr0niks/ink-proto/sprites/timo_v21.js` and `art/metr0niks/characters/tim-o/puppet/rig-v2-1.js`, and the v3 arms into `art/metr0niks/characters/tim-o/puppet/arms-v3.js` (sliced by `art/metr0niks/source/timo_arms_v3.py`; set its `SPEC` so a re-slice keeps the marks). Enemies go into `atlas.js`, `atlas.json` and the `SPEC` table in `art/metr0niks/source/ink_atlas.py`.
- **Apply targets**: Tim-o v4 marks go to `art/metr0niks/source/timo_exploded.py` (`TORSO_JOINTS`, or the seeding rules) and are re-sliced, then `art/metr0niks/source/timo_reassemble.py` registers the pieces back onto the hero frame and rewrites the joints, the knee pivots and the `hero` block of `exploded-v1.js` (the slicer alone writes seeded values only). `build.py` copies that block into the page as `ATLAS.rigs.timo4`, and `RIGS.timo4` reads its constants from it:
  - `restAim [F, B]`, `restBend [F, B]` (rad): the forearm direction and elbow bend of each arm in the hero frame. The front arm hangs there when `P.raise` is 0 and points along the aim when it is 1 (rest and walk 0, aim 1, stress sweeps). The far arm never aims (from the far shoulder it would run behind the torso): it keeps its hero bend and swings with the aim by `backFollow` (0.9) times `P.raise`, plus the walk swing `P.swing`. Replaces `backAim` for v4.
  - `legRest {hipF, hipB}`: each foot's rest offset from its hip in rig px per unit `legLen`. The feet are anchored to the ground line (bob and crouch move the body and bend the knees; the soles stay put). The hero draws the two soles staggered (the far one 25 hero px lower); `stagger` (lab constant, not from the data) keeps that share of it while standing: 1 = the hero's stance, the lowest sole on the line and the other above it; 0 = both soles on the line, the body sits lower and the longer leg bends. Walking blends the stagger out so both feet plant. `kneeRest {hipF, hipB}`: which side of the hip-to-foot line the hero draws each knee on (1 forward, -1 behind); the IK uses it while standing and bends forward once walking.
  - `legLen`, `legScales`, `armScale`, `packScale`: the pieces' own scales relative to the torso (the exploded edit grew the torso to .855 of the sheet and left the limbs smaller: front thigh and shin .75, back thigh .91, back shin .84, pack .85, arms 1.0). `legLen` is the front thigh's ratio and kr0's knob for the whole leg; `legScales {sprite: multiplier}` scales each other leg piece's bone and art relative to it. `packDx` replaces the code's 6 px pack nudge (0 for v4).
- **Rebuild the page** after the rig data changes: `python3 games/metr0niks/artifacts/puppet-rigging-lab/build.py`, then republish `puppet-rigging-lab.html` to the same artifact URL.
