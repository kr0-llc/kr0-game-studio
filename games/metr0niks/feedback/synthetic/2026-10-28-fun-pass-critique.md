# Metr0niks fun pass critique, Oct 28

Oct 28, 2026 · gdd-designer skill, critique mode · for kr0

kr0 asked for a critique of the proposed mechanics. This covers only the fun pass, GDD-D35 to GDD-D45, as written in [gdd.md](../../gdd.md) on PR #13 (commit f2e9975), read against the rest of the GDD, `sim.js` and GDD-D46. The [fun pass record](../../decisions/2026-10-28-fun-pass.md) was written by the same agent that wrote this critique, so treat this critique as a second look, not an independent review. Numbers worked out below are readings from the GDD's tables, not sim runs. Nothing in the GDD was edited for this critique.

## Summary

The proposals target the right weaknesses: levels 4 to 50 play alike, a Perfect is only a damage number, and there is no bet in extraction. Most of them are cheap and fit the pillars. Four rules can't be built as written, though. The GDD never says who gets credit for a kill, how damage multipliers stack, or whether Redline is something you choose, and the Wrecker's movement rules contradict each other. The most important design fix is that contracts and sector conditions come from the campaign seed. A player who dies replays the same levels with the same contracts and conditions, which undoes the variety they exist to add.

## Critical: blocks building the game

- **[Beat chain, Crossfire, Demolition bonus, Contracts] Nobody gets credit for a kill.** The chain pays on "an enemy killed while your chain is ×2…", Crossfire on "dies within 3 s", the demolition bonus on "killed by a collapse", and Crush and Repo man count kills. The GDD never says who killed an enemy. In co-op, if the host breaks the pillar and the guest's shot dropped the span, whose chain applies? Does a Wrecker's own charge, a Scuttler blast or an enemy-called Strike beacon count for anyone? **Fix:** add a Kill credit rule under Excursion rules: *"A kill belongs to the last mech that damaged the enemy within 3 s, or that destroyed the chunk whose collapse or spill killed it. Kills by enemy-made events (a Wrecker charge, a Scuttler blast, an enemy's Strike beacon) belong to nobody: they still drop the demolition bonus but count for no chain or contract."* Scrap stays physical, so the extra cubes go to whoever picks them up.
- **[Combat] Damage multipliers have no stacking order.** A hit can now carry power ÷ 100, the Mk level, the grade (×2, ×1, ×0.5), stagger (+50%), Crossfire (+50%), the Bulwark shield and Elite health. Only the scrap bonuses have a combine rule (Economy). **Fix:** one line in Combat: *"Damage = base × power ÷ 100 × upgrade × grade × (1 + stagger + Crossfire), rounded at the end; shields are checked first and block the whole hit."* Stagger and Crossfire then add like the scrap bonuses instead of compounding to ×2.25.
- **[Heat, Redline] Redline triggers on the default action.** Today, holding the minigun until 100 heat is what every new player does, and it costs a lockout. Under D36 the same hold now costs armor and a longer lockout, without the player ever choosing Redline. A heavy shot or a dash that pushes heat to 100 while the button happens to be held also starts it. "Every point of heat gained above 100" also doesn't say whether that is the gun's heat or heat net of drain. With the minigun those differ by 20 a second. **Fix:** make it a choice. *"At 100 heat the lockout starts. Release and press the light gun again within 0.3 s to cancel it and go into Redline."* Charge armor on net heat, and say whether Redline damage counts for the Nanite core's "3 s without damage" and for Untouchable.
- **[Enemies, Wrecker] The Wrecker's movement rules contradict each other.** It "keeps more than 8 m from every mech" and it "walks to the pillar or building chunk nearest a mech". The chunk nearest a mech is usually within 8 m of it. **Fix:** *"…walks to the support chunk nearest a mech that is at least 8 m from every mech; with none, it holds back and repositions."*

## Important: will cause rework or confusion

- **[Contracts, Sector conditions, Seed] Every climb repeats the same contracts and conditions.** Contracts and conditions come from the level seed. `sim.js` makes that seed from the campaign seed and the level (`levelSeed`), and the campaign seed stays 7 unless the player types a new one at level 1. So after a death, level 4 has the same condition and contract as last time, and so does every level after it. That works against the reason for D38 and D39, research principle 9: "something new on the climb back". **Fix:** count the climbs (each death or victory restart adds one) and mix that count into the draw for contracts, conditions and band picks, while the map and enemy landings stay on the campaign seed. Alternatively, re-roll the campaign seed on every new climb unless the player fixed one.
- **[Overtime] The choice is obvious at both ends of the campaign.** From the Economy and Waves tables, level 1 pays about 154 cubes over five waves. Overtime is wave 4 plus a loader, about 51 cubes, doubled to about 102, then +25% on everything: roughly 320 in all, twice the match, for a wave easier than wave 5. Early on, everyone will take it. At level 30, losing means going back to level 1, so nobody sane will. Either way the "bet" is only interesting in the middle. **Fix (kr0's call, it touches GDD-D14):** a death in Overtime loses all match scrap but doesn't reset the campaign: the level counts as cleared and you replay it. Then the bet is the wallet only, the way pillar 4 describes it. Otherwise, shrink the reward (drops ×1.5, no +25%) so it stays a real bet at level 1, and accept that late climbs skip it.
- **[Threat bands] Band enemies get lost in late waves.** With today's scaling, a level-40 wave 1 has about 32 bots. One Scuttler pack, one Bulwark and one Elite among them add almost nothing, which is the opposite of what a band should do. **Fix:** let band enemies replace scaled bots, for example "from band II, every fourth bot in a wave is a Scuttler pack". Or scale band counts at half the bot rate. The sweep that asks whether level 50 can be reached should run with this in.
- **[Breach charges, D41] Span drops become nearly free.** One charge destroys a whole 4×4×4 chunk, more than the bunker-buster's 3×3×3 hole, so two charges take out two pillars and drop a span every 8 s. With collapse damage scaling with level and the +50% demolition bonus, a charges-and-wait build looks like the best way to farm scrap. The overpass has only ten pillars, so a match also runs out of spans fast. **Fix:** a charge removes a 2 m sphere of voxels (pillars take two to three charges), or one charge stuck on a chunk, adding to the support rule's 20% instead of destroying it.
- **[Economy] Income goes up much more than spending.** Overtime, chain, demolition, Crossfire, Elites, contracts and Scrap caches all add scrap. Spending only gains six parts (about 525 scrap) and three upgrade tracks (390). The Oct 26 critique's question "what is scrap for once everything is owned?" now comes sooner. **Fix:** decide the target first ("runs to own everything", tuning history), then trim bonuses to fit. Or add one sink that grows without limit (for example paying for a reroll of the next level's contract or condition in the garage, which also makes the preview a decision).
- **[Bulwark] Flanking it is punished.** Line 405 says a Bulwark is staggered "only when the Perfect breaks its shield". So a Perfect from behind, the play the Bulwark exists to reward, does full damage but no stagger. It also doesn't say whether a shield-breaking pile-bunker Perfect deals damage on the same hit. **Fix:** "A Perfect from the front breaks the shield for 5 s and staggers; a Perfect from behind staggers as usual."
- **[Grav-skids, Overdrive core] Two parts break other rules.** "Floats over rubble" turns rubble, which blocks walking today, into nothing for one leg type, and the level design and AI pathing would have to treat that mech differently. "Floats over slag" makes slag harmless. Overdrive core with Redline lets you hurt yourself down to 35% armor to get +30 power. That may be fine, but it is a combo to watch, not an accident. **Fix:** Grav-skids cross slag at half damage and treat rubble as rubble. Note the Overdrive and Redline combo in the tuning questions.
- **[Waves] Wave contents are now defined in several places.** Waves says "This is the only place the wave structure is defined", but Scrap storm (+1 bot), Sniper's nest (two snipers), Pod rain, Cold front (fewer pods) and Elites all change wave contents elsewhere. Whether Overtime has an Elite in band V isn't stated. **Fix:** in Waves, say "sector conditions modify these waves (see Sector conditions)", and state Overtime's Elite.
- **[Pods] Fewer heals as fights get harder.** Field repair goes from 50% to 35% of pods just as threat bands raise the pressure. Strike beacon damage is "scaled like collapse damage", so it needs D41. **Fix:** keep Field repair at 50% and split the other 50% between the three boosts, or say the 35% is a starting value for the extract-rate sweep. Mark the D41 dependency.
- **[GDD-D46] The voxel focus changes two things.** Blackout's cost was a night look in three art styles. Under D46 only the voxel look is being built, so it is cheaper than the record says. Settings still offers three art styles, and the build order doesn't mention D46. **Fix:** update the record's cost for Blackout and ask kr0 whether Settings keeps Cinematic and Ink during the voxel focus.

## Polish: clarity, scanning, structure

- **Two versions of a rule in one sentence.** Extract ("1.4 s… with Overtime the 1.4 s becomes a 6 s choice"), the pod odds and collapse damage each state the old rule and the new one together, so a reader has to work out which is current. That is acceptable for a review draft, but once kr0 decides, rewrite each as one rule.
- **The cursor ring is overloaded.** It already shows heat, ATB charge and the timing ring, and now gains the chain number. Move the chain beside the heat bar, or onto the ring only while it is ×2 or more.
- **Hit-stop will pop.** Freezing only the struck enemy for 60 ms while the sim moves it will make it jump to its real position afterward. Freeze the impact effect and flash the enemy instead, or let it catch up over 60 ms.
- **Weak sector conditions.** Heat wave and Magnet fault only nudge a stat; they don't change a decision. Candidates: *Loose pillars* (every pillar starts with a cracked chunk), *Convoy* (a scrap truck crosses the map on wave 2 and pays out if stopped).
- **Contracts.** 20 scrap is about 13% of a level-1 match. That may be too little to make anyone take the 40-damage risk of Bring it down. Blitz's 75 s is untested. "Hot hands without Redline" means "if D36 is rejected", so say that.
- **Blueprints.** "Reach level 10" should say whether that means starting level 10 or extracting level 9. The Seismic hammer's 6 m sniper shake rarely fires, because the boss perches 8 to 16 m from a mech.
- **Cockpit voice.** "Span unstable (a span's last pillar is cracked)" needs a testable trigger, for example "a pillar holding a span has lost a chunk".
- **Edge cases to add.** Scrap cubes left on the floor when Overtime is called. A contract completed while the mech is down. Chain ×4 ATB bonus with Overclock (do they add or multiply?).

## Strengths

- Each proposal names the gap it fixes and the pillar it serves, and there is a build order that puts cheap, sim-only rules first.
- Overtime keeps GDD-D8. Blueprints answer research principle 9 without checkpoints, so pillar 5 holds.
- The zone props match the level design thread's layout in `sim.js`, down to the crane falling only when all four legs are broken.
- Hit-stop is presentation only, so co-op stays deterministic.
- The new counts are in Content counts, the terms in the glossary, and the sounds in the audio table.

## Open questions for the team

- Overtime: should a death in Overtime keep the level (the bet is scrap only), or reset the campaign like any death?
- Should every climb draw new contracts and conditions, and if so by a climb counter or a re-rolled seed?
- Redline: keep it as an opt-in gamble with a re-press, or drop D36?
- Should band enemies replace scaled bots so later bands feel different, rather than only harder?
- What economy target ("runs to own everything") should the scrap bonuses be trimmed to?
