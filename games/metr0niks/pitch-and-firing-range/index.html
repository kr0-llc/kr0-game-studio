Metr0niks

An isometric voxel mech brawler. You drive and shoot in real time. Heavy weapons charge on an ATB bar and hit on a timed release. Wreck the city, haul the scrap home, and rebuild your mech in the garage.

Why it's different

Build

Every part is a tradeoff

A bigger gun makes you slower and hotter. Lose a weapon in a fight and you're suddenly faster.

Destroy

The city is a weapon

Break two pillars and the overpass lands on whoever is under it.

Fight

Real-time driving, timed heavy hits

Steer and shoot freely. Heavy weapons charge on an ATB bar and land on a timed release.

Risk

Scrap only counts if you get out

Die and you lose the match's haul. Enemies race you for the drop pods that keep you alive.
