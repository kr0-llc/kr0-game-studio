| GDD-D7 | 2026-10-21 | Reword the *Gothic martial realism* pillar to match the art: "Armoured holy orders with halberds, rifles and jump packs, reliquaries and siege engines. | GDD-D6 | 2026-10-21 | Decide what ends a battle stalled at even node counts | (a) Late-match bleed: after a set time both sides bleed; (b) sudden-death node: one node becomes decisive late; (c) leave as is and rely on Wagers | Nothing ends it today: no bleed without a lead, no time limit, no tiebreak. | GDD-D5 | 2026-10-21 | Semester cut line: ship the 2D board, four modes, Sebastion ×3, the Coach and the Wager; defer the 3D view, authored maps, chosen Miracles, ranked rules and Tutorial Chapters II to VI | Ship a 3D view too; no cut line | A 15-week semester ends in one vertical slice; the 3D view is the most expensive item and adds no rules | Applied; kr0 to confirm | kr0 |
| GDD-D4 | 2026-10-21 | AI architecture lists three agents (Sebastion, Counsel, the Coach); difficulty is a property of Sebastion, described by what the player notices | Keep "Difficulty" as a fourth row | Difficulty is not an agent; describing play effect rather than computation is the doc's own aim | Applied | gdd-designer pass |
| GDD-D3 | 2026-10-21 | Grand Tactical is the default pace and the GDD quotes its values, with a Snappy column in one pace table.
