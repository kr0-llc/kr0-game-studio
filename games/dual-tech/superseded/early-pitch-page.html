Dual Tech

Chrono Trigger turns, hero-shooter kits: every attack ends in a timing hit, and two perfect hits fuse into a combo tech.

Core loop

  1. ATB bars fill; a ready hero picks Attack, Ability or Dual tech.
  2. The action resolves into a 1-second timing hit in that hero's style.
  3. Perfect hits build Sync; two ready heroes plus Sync unlock a dual tech.
  4. Dual tech = both heroes' timing hits back-to-back; land both for the big payoff.
  5. Win the encounter, pick 1 of 3 upgrades, move to the next fight.

Heroes

Brick Tank

Timing: hold to charge, release in the zone.

  • Shield slam: hold-release hit; perfect staggers and delays enemy ATB.
  • Barrier wall: taunt for 2 turns; perfect release adds a shield to all.

Vex Damage

Timing: stop a sweeping marker.

  • Railshot: one fast sweep; perfect pierces armor for 2x.
  • Flurry: three sweeps, each faster; damage per hit landed.

Lumen Support

Timing: rhythm taps on a beat.

  • Pulse heal: three on-beat taps; heal scales with taps hit.
  • Amp field: one tap on the downbeat; next ally hit gets a wider perfect zone.

Dual techs

  • Ricochet slam (Brick + Vex): release, then stop Vex's marker within 0.3s; rail bounces off the shield for 3x pierce.
  • Breach (Brick + Vex): perfect slam first, then a full Flurry; staggered target takes guaranteed crits.
  • Bulwark hymn (Brick + Lumen): release Brick's charge on Lumen's 4th beat; party shield plus heal over time.
  • Holy charge (Brick + Lumen): tap the beat while Brick holds; each tap adds charge, release for an AoE stun.
  • Overclock volley (Vex + Lumen): stop the marker on each beat for 4 shots; all perfect = ATB refill for both.
  • Prism shot (Vex + Lumen): Lumen taps once, Vex hits the same frame window; beam splits to every enemy.

Four-week plan

Week 1: prototype

  • ATB loop, turn menu, one dummy enemy.
  • Generic timing meter with perfect/good/miss.
  • Brick and Vex basic attacks.
  • Playable: win or lose one fight.

Week 2: kits

  • All three timing styles (hold, sweep, rhythm).
  • Six abilities and Sync meter.
  • First two dual techs end to end.

Week 3: content

  • Remaining four dual techs.
  • Five encounters, incl. one boss with a phase change.
  • Upgrade pick between fights.

Week 4: juice

  • Hit-stop, screen shake, combo name cards, SFX.
  • Balance pass on timing windows.
  • Title screen, results screen, build.

Cut if behind: drop upgrade picks and ship 4 dual techs (one per pair plus Ricochet slam and Prism shot), keeping all 5 encounters.
