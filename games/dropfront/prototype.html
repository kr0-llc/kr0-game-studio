Dropfront

Prototype of the core loop. One match against the enemy commander.

  • Every 15 seconds you draw 5 cards and refill 3 energy. The battle keeps running.
  • Click a card, then a highlighted point, to play it.
  • Click a point with enemies on it to drop in for 2 energy. You get 20 seconds: WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot. On touch, hold where you want to go and you fire automatically.
  • Gear cards equip your hero, up to 2 at a time, for every drop-in. Click equipped gear twice to discard it and make room.
  • While you fight, the map keeps running at 25% speed. Your hero then regroups for 20 seconds (40 if you went down).
  • The enemy plays the same tactic cards you do and drops its champion into fights. Drop into that point to face it.
  • Short on energy? Right-click or long-press a card to scrap it for +1, up to 2 per turn.
  • Held points score 2 per second. First to 2000 wins; at 8:00 the higher score wins.
