Basin command

Run the battleground with a hand of cards, then drop in and win the fight that decides it yourself.

Arathi Basin x Slay the Spire1-month soloSingle-player vs AI1 map, 5 points, 15 cards

Core loop

  1. Every 15 seconds a command turn starts: draw 5, refill 3 energy, the battlefield keeps running.
  2. Play squad cards to send bots down the roads; tactic cards bend the enemy commander's plans.
  3. When a capture meter hits 70%, the point flashes contested. Pay 2 energy to drop in for a 20-second top-down firefight.
  4. Gear cards are only playable while you are dropped in. Win the fight and the point locks for 10 seconds.
  5. Held points tick resources. First to 1600 wins the match; win 3 matches in a run, drafting 1 card after each.

3GearOrbital strike

Drop-in: mark an area, wipe it 3 seconds later. Exhaust.

The core tension: dropping in costs 2 of your 3 energy, so gear competes with squads. Combos: Feint pulls defenders, then Sabotage the point they left. Hold the line buys the seconds to Overclock into a fully geared drop-in. Rushers chain off whatever you just captured.

Four-week plan

Playable by Friday

Week 1: the two halves

  • Map graph, roads, bots walk and capture
  • Resource ticks and win at 1600
  • Top-down twin-stick arena with 20s timer
  • Hard-switch drop-in on a contested point

Week 2: the deck

  • Data-driven cards, draw, energy, discard
  • Targeting UI with valid-point highlights
  • All squad and tactic cards working
  • Enemy commander AI v1 (weighted point scoring)

Week 3: the firefight

  • Gear cards and 3 enemy bot types
  • Fight outcome feeds back to bot counts and meter
  • Juice: hit stop, screenshake, flip fanfare
  • Contested prompts and pause-free switching

Week 4: the run

  • 3-match run with card draft after each
  • AI difficulty ramp per match
  • Balance pass, sound, title and results screens
  • Build, playtest, fix

Cut if behind: drop the run and the draft; ship one match with a fixed 15-card deck.
