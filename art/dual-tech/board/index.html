Concept art · Dual Tech · Oct 2026

Dual Tech art board

The same JRPG drawn three ways: the prototype's flat blockout shapes, the chunky 16-bit pixel art of the original pitch, and a new cathedral stained-glass look that drops pixels entirely. Each row is one subject from the GDD: the three heroes, the four regular enemies, the scrap golem, the five-stop run, upgrades, the battle screen and a dual tech. Click any image to see it full size.

Style A

Prototype blockout

Plain flat rectangles, no outlines or texture, navy night and Chrono Trigger blue windows. What the playable fight looks like today.

Style B

Pitch pixel art

Chunky 16-bit sprites with navy outlines, starry sky, crescent moon and mountain silhouettes, gold pixel lettering. The original pitch look.

Style C

Stained glass

Art Nouveau cathedral glass: jewel-toned panes in thick lead lines, gold-leaf filigree, light shining through. No pixels at all.

Styles A and B were drawn in code (Python pixel and shape kits in art/dual-tech/source/) because the image credits were used up on Oct 22. Style C was generated with Scenario (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image). Build captures are screenshots of the current prototype; original pitch art comes from the pitch. Originals live in art/dual-tech/ by style, and every prompt is in art/dual-tech/prompts/.
