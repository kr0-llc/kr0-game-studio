Dual Tech is a short side-view JRPG with real-time turn bars in the spirit of Chrono Trigger, where every attack ends in a one-button timing hit and two heroes can chain hits into a 'dual tech'. The three heroes, always colour-coded: BRICK, a stocky tank in blue armour with a big pale tower shield (blue, square motif); VEX, a lean dark-haired gunslinger in red with a long rail rifle (red, diamond motif); LUMEN, a small hooded support mage in green robes with a floating golden orb that pulses to a beat (green, circle motif). Bottom HUD: a party panel with each hero's HP bar in their colour and a gold turn bar, a command menu window listing 'Shield slam', 'Barrier wall', 'Ricochet slam', and a horizontal timing bar with a sliding marker and a highlighted perfect zone.
