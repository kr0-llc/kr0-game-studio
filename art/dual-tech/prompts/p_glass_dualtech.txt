Dual Tech is a short side-view JRPG with real-time turn bars in the spirit of Chrono Trigger, where every attack ends in a one-button timing hit and two heroes can chain hits into a 'dual tech'. The three heroes, always colour-coded: BRICK, a stocky tank in blue armour with a big pale tower shield (blue, square motif); VEX, a lean dark-haired gunslinger in red with a long rail rifle (red, diamond motif); LUMEN, a small hooded support mage in green robes with a floating golden orb that pulses to a beat (green, circle motif). Key moment illustration of the dual tech 'RICOCHET SLAM': Brick braces and slams his tower shield forward while Vex fires a bright golden rail shot that ricochets off Brick's shield and pierces the scrap golem, which staggers with sparks flying. Lumen watches from behind. A banner reads 'RICOCHET SLAM!' with 'PERFECT + PERFECT' under it. No other text. ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO pixel art and NO flat blocks. Cathedral stained glass in the manner of Art Nouveau and Alphonse Mucha: every shape is a pane of luminous jewel-toned glass held by thick dark lead lines, with gold-leaf filigree borders, sweeping organic curves, halos and radiating light. Palette of sapphire blue, ruby red, emerald green and amber gold against deep violet. Light seems to shine through the image. Any UI is drawn as ornate leaded-glass panels and gilded frames with elegant hand-lettered Art Nouveau text. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
