Dual Tech is a short side-view JRPG with real-time turn bars in the spirit of Chrono Trigger, where every attack ends in a one-button timing hit and two heroes can chain hits into a 'dual tech'. The three heroes, always colour-coded: BRICK, a stocky tank in blue armour with a big pale tower shield (blue, square motif); VEX, a lean dark-haired gunslinger in red with a long rail rifle (red, diamond motif); LUMEN, a small hooded support mage in green robes with a floating golden orb that pulses to a beat (green, circle motif). Bottom HUD: a party panel with each hero's HP bar in their colour and a gold turn bar, a command menu window listing 'Shield slam', 'Barrier wall', 'Ricochet slam', and a horizontal timing bar with a sliding marker and a highlighted perfect zone. Keep text short and correctly spelled. ART STYLE: the original pitch art. Chunky 16-bit pixel art sprites with dark navy outlines and flat colour fills, visible square pixels, simple 2-tone shading. Indigo night sky full of tiny stars, a big pale crescent moon, layered dark blue mountain silhouettes, a slate-blue ground with darker pixel bricks. UI is a bright blue window with a lavender pixel border and chunky gold pixel lettering. Crisp, retro, charming, like a modern SNES-era game.
