Dual Tech is a short side-view JRPG with real-time turn bars in the spirit of Chrono Trigger, where every attack ends in a one-button timing hit and two heroes can chain hits into a 'dual tech'. The three heroes, always colour-coded: BRICK, a stocky tank in blue armour with a big pale tower shield (blue, square motif); VEX, a lean dark-haired gunslinger in red with a long rail rifle (red, diamond motif); LUMEN, a small hooded support mage in green robes with a floating golden orb that pulses to a beat (green, circle motif). Upgrade and item sheet: an 'upgrade pick' screen showing three upgrade cards to choose from, titled STEADY GRIP (blue, Brick), RESONANCE (green, Lumen) and QUICK SYNC (gold, shared). Around them, small item icons: a tower shield, a rail rifle, a golden beat orb, a gold SYNC meter gem, and grade badges reading PERFECT, GOOD, MISS. Keep text short and correctly spelled. ART STYLE: the current prototype blockout look. Every character and enemy is built from a few plain flat-coloured rectangles and squares, no outlines, no texture, no shading, no detail beyond simple blocks (a skin-coloured square head, a coloured box body, a rectangle shield or gun). Deep navy night background with a slightly lighter ground band and a few tiny gold star dots. UI in rounded blue gradient Chrono Trigger style windows with thin white borders and a small pixel display font. Clean, minimal, programmer-art but tidy.
