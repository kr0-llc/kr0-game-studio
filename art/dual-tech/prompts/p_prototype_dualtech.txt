Dual Tech is a short side-view JRPG with real-time turn bars in the spirit of Chrono Trigger, where every attack ends in a one-button timing hit and two heroes can chain hits into a 'dual tech'. The three heroes, always colour-coded: BRICK, a stocky tank in blue armour with a big pale tower shield (blue, square motif); VEX, a lean dark-haired gunslinger in red with a long rail rifle (red, diamond motif); LUMEN, a small hooded support mage in green robes with a floating golden orb that pulses to a beat (green, circle motif). Key moment illustration of the dual tech 'RICOCHET SLAM': Brick braces and slams his tower shield forward while Vex fires a bright golden rail shot that ricochets off Brick's shield and pierces the scrap golem, which staggers with sparks flying. Lumen watches from behind. A banner reads 'RICOCHET SLAM!' with 'PERFECT + PERFECT' under it. No other text. ART STYLE: the current prototype blockout look. Every character and enemy is built from a few plain flat-coloured rectangles and squares, no outlines, no texture, no shading, no detail beyond simple blocks (a skin-coloured square head, a coloured box body, a rectangle shield or gun). Deep navy night background with a slightly lighter ground band and a few tiny gold star dots. UI in rounded blue gradient Chrono Trigger style windows with thin white borders and a small pixel display font. Clean, minimal, programmer-art but tidy.
