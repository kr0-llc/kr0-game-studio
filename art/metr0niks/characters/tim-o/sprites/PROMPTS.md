# Tim-o sprite prompts

Every sheet below was made with GPT Image 2 (`model_openai-gpt-image-2`), 1536 × 1024, quality auto, one reference image: the side view of Tim-o cropped from `../sheet.png` (`side-reference.png`, Scenario asset `asset_RwZeqZVP1d8N4BpuKrcwn9f3`). Run, jetpack and explode came out of the Action Sprite Studio workflow; idle, dash and win were called directly (see `studio/design-workflows.md`).

Wrapper, used for idle, dash and win:

> Create a clean, professional 12-frame sprite sheet of the character in the reference image (Tim-o, a small robot with a jetpack tank on his back and an eye lens). Keep his colours, panels, eye lens and jetpack-tank shape identical to the reference in every frame. Plain solid white background divided by a sharp black 4 column by 3 row grid, no ground, no grass, no props, no numbers. Each frame is a side-view 2D platformer pose, the character FACES RIGHT in EVERY frame (the jetpack tank is always on his left, behind his back), contained in its cell and never touching the black lines. Same character height and scale in every cell. The hands are EMPTY in every frame: no weapon, no tool, nothing held, and the jetpack tank always stays fixed on his back. ANIMATION: *(action text)*

Action text:

- **idle:** IDLE loop with clearly visible, charming motion. 1 neutral stand; 2-4 big slow breath in, chest and shoulders rise, arms lift slightly; 5-6 head tilts curiously, eye lens looks up; 7 slow blink; 8-9 small sway forward with a shrug of both shoulders; 10-11 settle with a soft exhale, small steam puff from the back vent; 12 back to frame 1.
- **dash:** 1 neutral stand; 2-3 crouch low and lean far forward, both arms pulled back; 4-8 flung forward in a very low stretched dash, body nearly horizontal, both arms swept back with open hands, legs trailing, jetpack blasting an orange flame backwards, speed lines and a dust trail; 9-11 skid to a stop with dust; 12 neutral stand matching frame 1.
- **win:** WIN CELEBRATION, cute and joyful. 1 neutral stand; 2-3 crouch to load a jump; 4-5 happy hop high with both arms thrown up in a cheer, legs tucked, hearts and sparkles; 6 landing squash; 7-9 wiggle dance, body tilting left and right, one arm waving, eye lens squinting into a happy curve; 10 little spin pose; 11 tiny bow; 12 back to frame 1.
- **run, jetpack, explode:** workflow View & Action text, in the same frame-by-frame style (run cycle facing right; jetpack: crouch, ignite, rise with flame and smoke, hang in the air; explode: damage, fire, blast, debris, smoking wreck, one-shot).
