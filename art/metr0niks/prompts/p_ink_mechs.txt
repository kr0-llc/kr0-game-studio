Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. A mech character concept sheet on a neutral backdrop showing three player mech builds side by side in matching three-quarter view, each with a small hand-lettered name label: 1) 'BRAWLER' starter mech: minigun on the right arm, hydraulic pile-bunker spike on the left arm, vectored jump-jet legs, small reactor glowing on its back. 2) 'SIEGE' heavy mech: bulldozer siege treads instead of legs, huge bunker-buster cannon arm, twin-pod siege mortar on the shoulder, thick ablative slag-plating. 3) 'SKIRMISHER' light mech: slim long legs, plasma cutter arm, industrial magnetic winch cable arm, cryo-vent heatsink on the shoulder venting frost. No other text besides the three name labels. ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO voxels and NO cubes. Hand-drawn ligne-claire comic illustration in the spirit of French bande dessinee and 1970s science-fiction paperback art: confident clean black ink linework, flat watercolor washes, sun-bleached pastel palette of peach, sand, dusty coral, sage green and pale teal, bright midday desert sun, long soft shadows, lots of white paper breathing room, subtle paper grain. Mechs are elegant, organic, slightly whimsical walking machines with rounded hulls and painted insignia. Any UI is drawn as hand-lettered ink panels with screen-printed spot colors, like a printed comic or field manual. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
