Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Game screen mockup of the GARAGE and upgrade view: a mech stands on a repair lift in a workshop bay surrounded by robotic arms and welding sparks. Left panel shows five stat bars labeled POWER, ARMOR, SPEED, WEIGHT, HEAT and a list of six equipped slots: CORE, ARM-R, ARM-L, SHOULDER, LEGS, REACTOR with rarity colors. Right panel shows a blueprint of the selected part with a before/after stat preview, a SCRAP counter, and a large DROP IN button. Bottom strip of slot tabs. ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO voxels and NO cubes. Hand-drawn ligne-claire comic illustration in the spirit of French bande dessinee and 1970s science-fiction paperback art: confident clean black ink linework, flat watercolor washes, sun-bleached pastel palette of peach, sand, dusty coral, sage green and pale teal, bright midday desert sun, long soft shadows, lots of white paper breathing room, subtle paper grain. Mechs are elegant, organic, slightly whimsical walking machines with rounded hulls and painted insignia. Any UI is drawn as hand-lettered ink panels with screen-printed spot colors, like a printed comic or field manual. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
