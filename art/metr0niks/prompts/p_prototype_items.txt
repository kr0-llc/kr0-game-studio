Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. A game item and parts sheet laid out in a neat grid, each item presented as an isometric icon on its own tile with a small label: minigun, shotgun, plasma cutter, bunker-buster cannon, hydraulic pile-bunker, industrial mag-winch, twin-pod siege mortar, cryo-vent heatsink, vectored jump-jets, bulldozer siege treads, ablative slag-plating, reactor core. Tile frames color-coded by rarity: white, green, blue, purple, orange. A bottom row shows four drop pods (field repair, overclock, coolant flood, bolt-on plate) and a pile of scrap cubes (the currency). ART STYLE : clean flat-shaded blockout voxels, large chunky cubes, no surface textures, no grime, simple solid colors with soft ambient occlusion only. Palette: slate grey, charcoal, muted rust brown, mustard yellow, with bright cyan (#3fd6e8) glow accents and safety-orange (#e8722a) highlights on a near-black navy background.
