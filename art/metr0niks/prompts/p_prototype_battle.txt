Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Game screen mockup of the BATTLE view from a high-angle 45-degree isometric camera: the player mech fights patrol bots in city streets under a highway overpass. The player holds a heavy weapon timing ring: a shrinking circle closing on a green target ring around the mech. A falling slab of road casts a dark warning shadow on the ground. A cyan drop pod with a countdown marker is landing nearby, scrap cubes spill from a destroyed bot. HUD: armor bar, heat bar, ATB charge bar, WAVE 2/5 and SCRAP counter in the corners. Buildings nearest the camera are cut away in cross-section. ART STYLE : clean flat-shaded blockout voxels, large chunky cubes, no surface textures, no grime, simple solid colors with soft ambient occlusion only. Palette: slate grey, charcoal, muted rust brown, mustard yellow, with bright cyan (#3fd6e8) glow accents and safety-orange (#e8722a) highlights on a near-black navy background. Minimal, readable, toy-like, modern indie game look. Any UI uses thin cyan outlined panels on dark translucent navy with small uppercase monospace text. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
