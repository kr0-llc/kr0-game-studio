Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Environment concept art of the first map, 'Overpass Sector 4': dense slum streets and shacks underneath a massive elevated six-lane highway overpass held up by thick concrete pillars. Armored transport trucks drive on the highway above. One section of highway between two broken pillars is mid-collapse, falling onto the street below and casting a dark shadow on the ground. Scrap cubes scattered in the rubble. High-angle isometric view, establishing shot, no characters in focus. ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO voxels and NO cubes. Hand-drawn ligne-claire comic illustration in the spirit of French bande dessinee and 1970s science-fiction paperback art: confident clean black ink linework, flat watercolor washes, sun-bleached pastel palette of peach, sand, dusty coral, sage green and pale teal, bright midday desert sun, long soft shadows, lots of white paper breathing room, subtle paper grain. Mechs are elegant, organic, slightly whimsical walking machines with rounded hulls and painted insignia. Any UI is drawn as hand-lettered ink panels with screen-printed spot colors, like a printed comic or field manual. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
