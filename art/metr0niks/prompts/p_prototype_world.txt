Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Environment concept art of the first map, 'Overpass Sector 4': dense slum streets and shacks underneath a massive elevated six-lane highway overpass held up by thick concrete pillars. Armored transport trucks drive on the highway above. One section of highway between two broken pillars is mid-collapse, falling onto the street below and casting a dark shadow on the ground. Scrap cubes scattered in the rubble. High-angle isometric view, establishing shot, no characters in focus. ART STYLE : clean flat-shaded blockout voxels, large chunky cubes, no surface textures, no grime, simple solid colors with soft ambient occlusion only. Palette: slate grey, charcoal, muted rust brown, mustard yellow, with bright cyan (#3fd6e8) glow accents and safety-orange (#e8722a) highlights on a near-black navy background. Minimal, readable, toy-like, modern indie game look. Any UI uses thin cyan outlined panels on dark translucent navy with small uppercase monospace text. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
