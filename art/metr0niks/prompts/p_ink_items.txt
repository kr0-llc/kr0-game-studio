Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. A game item and parts sheet laid out in a neat grid, each item presented as an isometric icon on its own tile with a small label: minigun, shotgun, plasma cutter, bunker-buster cannon, hydraulic pile-bunker, industrial mag-winch, twin-pod siege mortar, cryo-vent heatsink, vectored jump-jets, bulldozer siege treads, ablative slag-plating, reactor core. Tile frames color-coded by rarity: white, green, blue, purple, orange. A bottom row shows four drop pods (field repair, overclock, coolant flood, bolt-on plate) and a pile of scrap cubes (the currency). ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO voxels and NO cubes. Hand-drawn ligne-claire comic illustration in the spirit of French bande dessinee and 1970s science-fiction paperback art: confident clean black ink linework, flat watercolor washes, sun-bleached pastel palette of peach, sand, dusty coral, sage green and pale teal, bright midday desert sun, long soft shadows, lots of white paper breathing room, subtle paper grain. Mechs are elegant, organic, slightly whimsical walking machines with rounded hulls and painted insignia. Any UI is drawn as hand-lettered ink panels with screen-printed spot colors, like a printed comic or field manual. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
