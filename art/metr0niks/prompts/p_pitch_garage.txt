Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Game screen mockup of the GARAGE and upgrade view: a mech stands on a repair lift in a workshop bay surrounded by robotic arms and welding sparks. Left panel shows five stat bars labeled POWER, ARMOR, SPEED, WEIGHT, HEAT and a list of six equipped slots: CORE, ARM-R, ARM-L, SHOULDER, LEGS, REACTOR with rarity colors. Right panel shows a blueprint of the selected part with a before/after stat preview, a SCRAP counter, and a large DROP IN button. Bottom strip of slot tabs.
