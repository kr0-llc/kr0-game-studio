Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. Game screen mockup of the BATTLE view from a high-angle 45-degree isometric camera: the player mech fights patrol bots in city streets under a highway overpass. The player holds a heavy weapon timing ring: a shrinking circle closing on a green target ring around the mech. A falling slab of road casts a dark warning shadow on the ground. A cyan drop pod with a countdown marker is landing nearby, scrap cubes spill from a destroyed bot. HUD: armor bar, heat bar, ATB charge bar, WAVE 2/5 and SCRAP counter in the corners. Buildings nearest the camera are cut away in cross-section. ART STYLE: a completely different take with NO voxels and NO cubes. Hand-drawn ligne-claire comic illustration in the spirit of French bande dessinee and 1970s science-fiction paperback art: confident clean black ink linework, flat watercolor washes, sun-bleached pastel palette of peach, sand, dusty coral, sage green and pale teal, bright midday desert sun, long soft shadows, lots of white paper breathing room, subtle paper grain. Mechs are elegant, organic, slightly whimsical walking machines with rounded hulls and painted insignia. Any UI is drawn as hand-lettered ink panels with screen-printed spot colors, like a printed comic or field manual. Keep any text short, legible and correctly spelled.
