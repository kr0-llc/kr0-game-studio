Tim-o item parts sheets, v1 (28 Oct 2026, for the ink item lab, games/metr0niks/prototypes/item-lab.html)

Tool: Scenario, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst (model_openai-gpt-image-2-5-sunburst), 1536 x 1024, quality medium, background transparent, one image per sheet.
References (in this order): asset_Rzs92LmBTZFho7qvgetHLigv (parts sheet v2.1, for scale, camera and line) and asset_fgDKMUX8fgmRfwyzQSSFqv2s (four-view turnaround, Tim-o's main reference).
Five runs, about 4 credits each (about 20 in all). Previews: ../characters/tim-o/items/sheet-<name>.webp. Sliced by ../source/timo_items_atlas.py.
The minigun, pile-bunker, Reactor Mk1 and jump-jet legs (and the bare torso, "standard frame") are the v2.1 parts themselves; these sheets draw the other 17 of the 21 garage parts from sim.js (MetroSim.PARTS).
