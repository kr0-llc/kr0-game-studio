Metr0niks is an isometric voxel mech brawler set in a ruined industrial city. Players drive salvaged mechs built from six slots (core, right arm, left arm, shoulder, legs, reactor), shoot in real time, fire heavy weapons on a timing ring, and topple buildings for scrap cubes. A mech character concept sheet on a neutral backdrop showing three player mech builds side by side in matching three-quarter view, each with a small hand-lettered name label: 1) 'BRAWLER' starter mech: minigun on the right arm, hydraulic pile-bunker spike on the left arm, vectored jump-jet legs, small reactor glowing on its back. 2) 'SIEGE' heavy mech: bulldozer siege treads instead of legs, huge bunker-buster cannon arm, twin-pod siege mortar on the shoulder, thick ablative slag-plating. 3) 'SKIRMISHER' light mech: slim long legs, plasma cutter arm, industrial magnetic winch cable arm, cryo-vent heatsink on the shoulder venting frost.
