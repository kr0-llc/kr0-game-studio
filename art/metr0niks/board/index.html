Metr0niks art board

The same game drawn three ways: the prototype's clean blockout voxels, the gritty micro-voxel look of the pitch art, and a sunlit hand-inked comic style that drops voxels entirely. Each row is one subject from the GDD. Click any image to see it full size.

Style A

Prototype blockout

Big flat-shaded cubes, no texture, slate and rust with cyan glass and orange armor. What the live build looks like today.

Style B

Pitch rust and sparks

Dense micro-voxels, chipped rust paint, hazard stripes, night lighting, sparks and smoke. The original pitch concept look.

Style C

Sun-bleached ink

Ligne-claire ink and flat watercolor, midday sun, peach, sand, coral and sage. No voxels at all; rounded, slightly whimsical machines.

Concept images were made with Figma AI (Gemini 3.1 image). Build captures are screenshots of the current prototype. Originals live in art/metr0niks/ by style. Small thumbnails under a cell are build captures or original pitch art, for comparison.
