# Blackletter: Chronos, art and screenshots

Collected 2026-10-21 from the game's repo (`kr0/blackletter-chronos`, commit 0a27c17, tuning v2.14.6) and from a local run of the build. The card art is the official *Blackletter* tabletop art: white ink silhouettes on black, blackletter titles, a stat column down the right. The figures are armoured holy soldiers with halberds, rifles and jump packs.

## In this folder

| Folder | What | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cards/units/` | Printed unit cards (600 dpi): Chopper, Jumper, Shooter | 3 |
| `cards/upgrades/` | Printed upgrade cards: Heavy, Elite, Horde, Steadfast, Devastator, Raider | 6 |
| `cards/backs/` | Unit and upgrade card backs | 2 |

| `brand/` | Canterbury blackletter masks (ℬ mark, host sigils S and C, muster and Codex titles, victory seal) and the app icon | 8 |
